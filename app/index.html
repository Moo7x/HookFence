Hand this basket to someone else

They get full control: they can take the tokens out, and you no longer can. Anyone you allowed to manage it is removed automatically. This cannot be undone from your side.

Copy this mix

Creates a new basket for you with the same split, bought with your own money. Nothing is taken from the original and its owner keeps everything.

Take the tokens out

Sends the actual tokens to your wallet and closes the basket. Nothing is sold, so this works even when prices are unavailable — on a weekend, for example.
