Tokenised baskets

Jayo

Build a basket of tokens with one payment, own it as a single position you can hand to someone else, copy anyone's mix with your own money, and take the actual tokens out whenever you want.

Everything here is a test. This runs on Robinhood Chain testnet. The tokens have no value and cannot be redeemed for anything — not real stock, not real dollars. Nothing you do here can gain or lose money.

0 Get set up

Three things before your first basket. All of them are free test assets.

  1. Connect a browser wallet. It will ask to add or switch to Robinhood Chain testnet (chain 46630). Every transaction is signed in your wallet; this site never sees a key.
  2. Get test ETH for fees. Each action here costs well under 0.0001 test ETH. Free from a faucet: Chainlink faucet · QuickNode faucet.
  3. Get test rUSDG to buy with. rUSDG is a public test stablecoin on this network (not Paxos USDG, and not Jayo's) that anyone can mint for free.
  4. Jayo's spending permission.

    Copy this mix

    Creates a new basket for you with the same split, bought with your own money. Nothing is taken from the original and its owner keeps everything.

    Take the tokens out

    Sends the actual tokens to your wallet. Nothing is sold, so this works even when prices are unavailable — on a weekend, for example. You can take part of it and keep the basket, or take everything and close it.
