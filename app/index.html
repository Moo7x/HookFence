      How a Jayo basket works

      It is yours, token by token

      The basket is a single token in your wallet. The contract records exactly how much of each Stock Token it holds, and nobody else's holdings are mixed in.

      Adding to it

      Money added to a basket is split by its plan and bought into it; Stock Tokens can be moved in directly. Every addition names the owner shown on the page and is refused if the basket has changed hands.

      Take out what you want, when you want

      Withdraw one Stock Token, a share of everything, or all of it. Withdrawing needs no price, so it works even when buying is paused. Taking out the last holding closes the basket.

      Where the prices come from, and what they protect
      What is real here, and what is a test

      This runs on Robinhood Chain testnet. The TSLA and AMZN tokens are test versions of Stock Tokens, and the Uniswap v4 pools are real testnet pools. None of it has value, and none of it can be exchanged for anything real. rUSDG is a public test stablecoin that anyone can mint. It is not Paxos USDG.

      On mainnet, Stock Tokens give economic exposure to a company's shares; they are not the shares, and they carry no shareholder rights. They are not offered to US persons. Jayo holds Stock Tokens; it does not hold shares.

      Jayo's own contracts are not audited. The code, tests and every transaction behind this page are public: source on GitHub.
