1 Create a basket

Fund with USDG. The contract buys each asset through Uniswap v4 and an ERC-721 holds the result. Preview first — those are reference amounts, not fills.

2 Actual holdings

What the position really owns, read from the contract — not what was quoted.

3 Transfer, and prove prior authority dies

Appoint a manager, transfer the position, then confirm the old owner and their delegate can no longer control it.

4 Copy the allocation

Creates a new position with the same recipe, funded entirely by you. No holdings, ownership or history are copied.

5 Redeem in kind, with pricing unavailable

Make every feed stale — a weekend, in effect. Creating anything becomes impossible. Redemption still works, because it never asks for a price.
